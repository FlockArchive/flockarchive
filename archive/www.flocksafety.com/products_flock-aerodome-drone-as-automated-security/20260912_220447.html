FAQ

What is Flock Aerodome Drone as Automated Security (DAS), and how does it work for private sites?

An all-in-one, “drone-in-a-box” security system that ties into your alarms and sensors. When an alert fires, an operator can one-click launch to the exact spot and view live HD, color night vision, or thermal video from anywhere. It’s built for large footprints and remote ops.

How fast is the response?

Launch happens in moments and on-scene times are typically “seconds,” depending on site size. For context, our public-safety Drone as First Responder (DFR) programs average ~86 seconds city-wide; private sites are often smaller/faster.

Can it fly scheduled perimeter patrols?

Patrols can be flown today, but scheduled/recurring patrol automation is a near-term software update. We’ll design safe patrol routes with you.

What about privacy for employees and visitors?

Flock Aerodome flies on your property for defined security purposes (alerts/patrols), with access-controlled video, no facial recognition, and policy/SOP guidance for transparent, ethical use.

Which properties get the most value?

Sprawling retail centers, warehouses/logistics yards, healthcare campuses, railyards/transportation, and corporate or college campuses—anywhere guards can’t be everywhere at once.

Does it auto-launch on alarms?

Today, alerts can trigger a one-click workflow, and the drone flies the route automatically after operator confirmation. Full “auto-dispatch” depends on FAA approvals and is on our roadmap.

Is it safe to operate over my property?

Yes. We use geofencing, obstacle avoidance, airspace awareness, and documented failsafes, plus site surveys and waivers. Our approach is to reduce risk versus sending a guard in blind.

What hardware is included?

Enterprise-grade dock and drone with HD/thermal/color night vision, ruggedized enclosures—built for continuous outdoor security operations.
