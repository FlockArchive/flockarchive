FAQ

How are video cameras powered?

Flock’s video cameras come in AC or solar-powered options. Our team can help you select the best camera for your specific needs.

What AI features do Flock’s video cameras have?

Flock’s video cameras are equipped with advanced AI analytics features including Guardian Mode, People Detection Alerts, Visual Alerts and Flock FreeForm.

Can I install video cameras and LPR cameras on the same pole?

Yes, both devices can be installed on the same pole.

What is the difference between the single lens and dual lens PTZ camera?

Single lens PTZs provide a single POV. The dual lens PTZ delivers two POVs with a picture-in-picture user experience. This includes one 90 degree wide-view angle and a 32x zoom that stream simultaneously.

How does Flock’s subscription model work?

Flock’s all-in-one subscription makes it affordable to get started. One simple annual price covers hardware, software, maintenance, and support with no hidden fees. Optional features like alert response service can be added as needed.

How can I collaborate with law enforcement using this system?

Flock makes it easy to securely share your cameras with over thousands partner agencies that are already on the platform. You can loop in law enforcement in real time to support faster, more coordinated responses.
