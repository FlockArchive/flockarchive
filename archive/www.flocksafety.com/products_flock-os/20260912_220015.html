FAQ

Who is FlockOS for?

FlockOS is designed for any team that needs real-time situational awareness, including police and fire departments, fusion centers, global security operations centers (GSOC), hospitals, logistics hubs, and private sector agencies. Whether you operate from a dedicated operations center, or a mobile phone in the field, FlockOS gives everyone the same live map, data, and tools to stay informed, connected, and ready to respond.

What makes FlockOS different from a VMS or CAD system?

FlockOS is not just a video viewer or dispatch tool, it’s the glue that connects all your tech. While CAD, AVL, and VMS systems are valuable, they often live in silos. FlockOS integrates them into one real-time map, layering alerts, video, sensors, and location data so your team sees the full picture and can act immediately with context and coordination.

Can we use FlockOS without a Real-Time Crime Center (RTCC) or GSOC?

Yes. FlockOS doesn’t require a dedicated facility or a big-city tech stack. Any screen can serve as your command center—desktop, tablet, or mobile. Whether you're a small agency, private security team, or regional task force, FlockOS brings all your data together so you can operate smarter, respond faster, and stay connected across locations and roles.

Is FlockOS only for police departments?

No. FlockOS supports a wide range of public safety and security teams, including fire, EMS, and private security. Whether you’re coordinating a response, managing an event, or monitoring your business locations, FlockOS gives every team the tools they need to see what’s happening and act fast.
