FAQ

What do I get when I purchase a Flock camera?

You get access to the full Flock Safety Platform, including real-time alerts, mobile tools, investigative software, and optional sharing the national LPR network.

Is the mobile app free for users?

Yes. The Flock Safety mobile app is free, providing officers and security teams with access to alerts and vehicle data on the go.

Is the platform included with my camera purchase?

Yes. All LPR camera purchases include access to the Flock Safety Platform and core features—no additional subscription required.

Can businesses or HOAs use this platform?

Yes. The platform supports public agencies, private security teams, HOAs, and commercial partners alike.
