Public safety works better together

Flock brings together the tools that organizations rely on every day, helping law enforcement, schools, businesses, and neighborhoods respond faster, investigate more effectively, and work together from the same platform.

Raising the bar

Building technology responsibly means never standing still. We continually improve privacy, accountability, and security guardrails across the products communities rely on.

This year, we've:

Reduced our recommended default data retention from 30 days to 7 days

Given customers more control over how data is shared across agencies

Expanded audit requirements and proactive monitoring to detect misuse

Made multi-factor authentication mandatory for every customer

Increased transparency around data ownership, security, and agency policies

Because protecting communities also means protecting civil liberties.
