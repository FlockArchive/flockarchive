FAQ

What types of sounds can the system detect?

In addition to gunshots, Flock Gunshot and Audio Detection can detect fireworks, sideshows, and other disruptive community events. Our detection library is continually expanding to include emerging threats that are relevant to your team’s specific needs.

How does audio detection work with video and LPR?

Flock Gunshot and Audio Detection connects audio signals with footage and license plate data through FlockOS. This enables a multi-angle view of incidents, allowing agencies to confirm threats, identify vehicles, and act quickly with the whole picture in view.

Is the system always recording or listening?

No. Flock Gunshot and Audio Detection is event-triggered and policy-governed. It activates only when it detects an acoustic signature of interest, such as a gunshot or a sideshow, ensuring your agency remains compliant with legal and privacy standards.

Can Flock gunshot and audio detection work as a standalone audio solution?

Yes, but it’s most powerful when part of a connected system. Used in conjunction with video, LPR, and CAD, Flock Gunshot and Audio Detection becomes a force multiplier, providing operators with deeper context, reducing response times, and supporting more effective investigations.
